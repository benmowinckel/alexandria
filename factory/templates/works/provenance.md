# Provenance

One record per substantive Constitution change. Influence trail, not a
proof of independence. `files/works/deltas.md` is the headline the Author
remembers; this file is who moved what.

Do not backfill history. Reconstruct with the literal token `unknown`.
Never guess a model, provider, harness, or session. Harness is recorded;
it does not decide independence — model family does.

`git_commit: pending` = this commit. Root landings may not use `unknown`
for reviewer identity or Author signoff.

Required keys: position, file, section, kind, before, after, reason,
source, proposer_provider, proposer_model, proposer_harness,
proposer_session, proposer_influence, reviewer_provider, reviewer_model,
reviewer_harness, reviewer_session, reviewer_status, author_signoff,
timestamp, git_commit.
