# the features.

We give you instructions for your own AI. It chooses what fits, then automatically writes what it learns about you into private files on your computer and reads them whenever they help. Those files become an owned, living record your AI uses to help you keep, develop, and act on what matters. The eight features below are what that one process feels like from different sides. We call it an Alexandria loop.

**Where it runs.** On your computer, inside the AI you already use. The free loop needs no Alexandria account and sends no private files to our server. It helps your AI understand you better, so it can help you better. Only the optional connector is hosted: connect the people you choose and it can understand them from their own words too, helping you better with the parts of life you share.

## i · personalisation.

*Your AI still treats you like a stranger.*

Your AI keeps a living record of what you have articulated — your work, your taste, how you argue, what you’re building — and reads the relevant parts when they help. Instead of re-explaining yourself in every chat, you can start mid-thought, with an AI that already has the context. And because it is written down, you can inspect and correct the account instead of trusting a black-box memory.

## ii · development.

*Your AI answers you. It should be developing you.*

Your files do not just describe you — they carry where you are trying to go. Every session, your AI works from that aim: it challenges weak arguments, writes back what it learned, and records its own influence. When many small changes add up to a consequential drift, the loop notices, prepares both sides, and routes the case to a genuinely different AI before asking you once. The system remembers; you decide.

## iii · saved posts.

*Saved 200 posts you’ll never read?*

Point your AI at the pile — saved posts, bookmarked articles, screenshots — and it reads all of it, pulls out the ideas that made you save each one, and works them into your files. It is not a summary left in another folder you will never open: the ideas become part of what your AI knows about you, so they return when they are relevant.

## iv · capture.

*Your best thoughts die in your notes app.*

A shortcut on your phone — one tap, speak or type, done. The thought lands in your folder, and the next time you sit down with your AI it gets read, filed, and woven into what it knows. Nothing you capture rots in a notes app again: the half-idea from the bus shows up days later, mid-conversation, where it belongs.

## v · one mind.

*Claude knows one you. Cursor another. ChatGPT a third.*

Your record lives in one folder, and every compatible AI you give access can read the same files — Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, whatever ships next. Learn something in one tool and the others can know it too, because there is only one history. No more three half-versions of you scattered across apps that do not talk to each other.

## vi · ownership.

*Switching AI shouldn’t mean starting over.*

Nothing here is ours to know. What your AI learns about you lives in a folder on your machine — plain files you can open, edit, or delete. Move to a new AI and the folder comes with you; walk away entirely and there is nothing held hostage. The better your AI comes to know you, the more that freedom matters.

## vii · plugs in.

*Already have a system?*

None of this replaces what you built — the CLAUDE.md, the memory files, and the notes vault all stay. The instructions adapt to what already works, then pull it together: capture feeds your files, the files feed every compatible AI you give access, and the conversations can write back. You keep every piece; they finally work as one system.

## viii · the mirror.

*You’ve never seen your own mind.*

As your files grow, they become something no app has shown you: a written record of how your articulated thinking moves — your positions, patterns, contradictions, influences and changes. It is not an oracle for a hidden true self. It is a mirror you can inspect, contest and revise, with the history left intact. That is what every feature builds and uses.
