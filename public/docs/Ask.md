# before you start.

This is the public context behind the ask page for someone deciding whether to try the free Alexandria loop. Starting the loop is a small, reversible test. It is not a decision to join the community, publish anything, replace the tools they use, or hand private files to Alexandria.

## What is the loop?

An Alexandria loop is a method for helping the ai a person already uses build a deeper, lasting understanding of them. The ai keeps one living record in ordinary files on the person’s own computer, reads that record when it would help, and improves it as they work together. The person can read, correct, move, or remove those files.

The point is simple: they stop starting from zero. A useful thought can return later. A new ai can pick up the same history. The record stays inspectable and portable instead of being trapped inside one company’s memory system.

## What happens when someone starts?

The start page first asks what they already use. It adapts to a computer-reaching agent such as Codex, Claude Code, or Cursor, or to a normal chat such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

With a computer-reaching agent, the person copies a short request asking their own ai to inspect the public project and decide whether the method fits their existing setup. Nothing is installed or changed during that review. If the ai recommends proceeding, it must explain the next step and wait for clear approval before changing anything.

The loop is meant to fit around what the person already has. Existing instructions, notes, memory files, and working habits stay in place unless the person and their own ai deliberately decide otherwise. Starting does not require joining the community.

They can stop later. The included uninstaller disconnects Alexandria from supported ai tools while keeping the person’s files by default. The person can separately remove those files too if they want them gone.

## Does it work with the ai they already use?

Yes, if that ai can keep a durable record somewhere it can read again. A computer-reaching agent can use the full local files. A normal chat can begin with its own instructions and a writable file home, then connect to the local loop later. The start page shows the honest path for the tool the person chooses; it does not pretend a chat-only setup has local computer access.

Alexandria is not another ai to switch to. The person keeps using their current ai. The method is portable, so they can add another ai later without rebuilding their history from scratch.

## How is it different from ordinary ai memory?

Ordinary product memory is useful, but it is usually partial and locked inside one service. An Alexandria loop gives the person one record they can inspect, shape, keep, and let different ais use.

It also goes beyond remembering facts. When a useful thought exists only in the person’s head, they can start a deliberate Alexandria session. Their ai asks questions, tests gaps or contradictions, and saves only what the person confirms. The work of articulating the thought remains theirs; the loop makes that work compound.

## What stays private?

The private loop stays in files on the person’s own computer. Alexandria’s server does not receive those private files. Account connection does not read or send them. Publishing, joining the community, connecting other people, and every other public action are separate choices with separate permission.

The person’s own ai necessarily sees whatever they choose to show it under that ai provider’s normal terms. Alexandria does not erase that provider relationship or make a blanket promise about another company’s systems.

## Do they need to be technical?

No. The person chooses the ai they already use, copies one clear request, and lets that ai inspect the method. Their ai handles the files and explains any proposed change before making it. The person’s job is to decide what they want and to think out loud when something worth keeping has never been written down.

## Why start now?

The record is made from time. A future model may infer a great deal, but it cannot perfectly recover what changed, what influenced a decision, or what the person nearly chose and never said. Starting earlier gives later intelligence a better history to work from.

The decision is still small: try the free loop, keep it if it helps, and remove it if it does not. The optional community can wait.

## Cost and next step

The private loop is free and open. The community is optional and separate: it is free for 30 days, then $30 a month; it stays free while three people who joined through that member remain members, and the founder will waive the price for someone who cannot afford it. None of that is part of starting the private loop.
