If the homepage made sense, this is the rest — what still sits between you and starting.

An alexandria loop is an idea: a method of working with your own ai, not a piece of software. The founder made his free and open. The button is a recipe you hand to it — what the loop is, why it matters, how to make yours, and his as the reference. Your ai merges whatever you already have — a CLAUDE.md, memory files, notes, a vault, a half-built loop — so the loop is closed and good, however close you already were. It writes plain files on your computer as it learns how you think, and reads them whenever they help. No profile to fill in. Most of it runs while you work.

Two halves. Passive: use ai as you already do — it quietly writes, then quietly reads. Active: when something useful only exists in your head, turn on a deliberate session. It asks what it has been wondering, points at gaps and contradictions, and pushes until you have said what you actually think. You talk. It writes the result into the same files.

Week one is practical. You stop re-explaining yourself. An idea from Tuesday can return on Friday. A new ai can pick up where the last one stopped, because the history is yours — no company locks you in.

Over months, the job itself shifts. Without the map, a useful thought helps once and disappears. With it, your ai brings that thought back, develops it, connects it to new work, and helps you act on it — instead of only answering.

That power cuts both ways. The same access that develops you can shape the aims it later helps you pursue. Alexandria does not pretend the influence can be removed. It makes it attributable and contestable: one owned history you can open, plural models when a choice may deserve exceptional protection, forced opposition, reversible changes. More resistance than one private optimiser with no inspectable past.

The usual worries are covered. Private files stay on your computer; our server never receives them. Everything you already use keeps working. Ordinary markdown. Delete the folder and the loop is gone.

The free loop is complete alone. Joining the community is optional — library, marketplace, shared craft, when you want them. The connector is the only paid part. You choose what to publish; private files stay private.

One reason not to wait: the map is made of time. A later model may describe you brilliantly, but it cannot backfill what changed, what influenced it, and what you chose. Starting today and meaning to start are not the same decision later.

Press the button. Show your ai the instructions and decide together.
