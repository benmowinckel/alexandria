# alexandria, plainly.

If the homepage made sense, this is what still sits between you and the button.

You get the founder’s complete instructions free. Press start, show them to your ai, and decide together what to keep. Your ai merges whatever you already have — a CLAUDE.md, memory files, notes, a vault, a half-built loop — into a full alexandria loop in one go. It makes plain files on your computer and continually writes what it learns about how you think. Later it reads those files whenever they help. You do not fill in a profile. Most of it runs while you work.

Two halves. Passive: use ai as you already do, and it quietly writes and quietly reads. Active: when something useful only exists in your head, turn on a deliberate session. It asks what it has been wondering, points at gaps and contradictions, and pushes until you have said what you actually think. You talk. It writes the result into the same files.

Week one, the difference is practical. You stop re-explaining yourself. An idea from Tuesday can return on Friday. A new ai can pick up where the last one stopped, because the history is yours, not locked in one product.

Over months, the bigger shift is what the ai’s job becomes. Without the map, a useful thought helps once and disappears. With it, your ai brings that thought back, develops it, connects it to new work, and helps you act on it — instead of only answering.

That power cuts both ways. The same access that develops you can shape the aims it later helps you pursue. Alexandria does not pretend the influence can be removed. It makes it attributable and contestable: one owned history you can open, plural models when a choice may deserve exceptional protection, forced opposition, reversible changes. That is more resistance than one private optimiser with no inspectable past.

The worries that usually stop people here are covered. Your private files stay on your computer; our server never receives them. Everything you already use keeps working. The files are ordinary markdown. Delete the folder and the loop is gone.

The free loop is complete alone. Joining the others — library, marketplace, shared craft — is optional. The connector is the only paid part. You choose what to publish; private files stay private.

One reason not to wait: the map is made of time. A later model may describe you brilliantly, but it cannot backfill what changed, what influenced it, and what you chose. Starting today and meaning to start are not the same decision later.

Press the button. Show your ai the instructions and decide together.
